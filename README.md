## Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales performance using the Superstore Sales Dataset.

The objective is to understand sales trends, identify top-performing products, compare category and regional performance, and evaluate profitability. The analysis is presented through Python-based data analysis and a client-ready business dashboard.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following business questions:

1. Which products generate the most revenue?
2. How do sales change over time?
3. Which categories perform strongly?
4. Which regions generate higher sales?
5. Which categories contribute more to profitability?
6. Where should the business focus to support future growth?

---

## Dataset

The **Superstore Sales Dataset** contains sales transaction information including:

- Order and shipping details
- Customer information
- Product details
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Category and sub-category
- Regional information

The original dataset was cleaned and prepared before analysis.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Focus on high-performing products**  
   Continue promoting products that contribute strongly to overall sales and monitor their demand regularly.

2. **Strengthen high-performing categories**  
   Allocate suitable marketing and sales resources to categories showing strong sales and profitability.

3. **Review lower-profit areas**  
   Analyze pricing, discounts, product costs, and sales strategies for categories with relatively lower profitability.

4. **Use regional performance for planning**  
   Compare regional performance to identify opportunities for targeted marketing and business expansion.

5. **Monitor sales trends regularly**  
   Track sales over time to identify changes in demand and support better sales and inventory planning.

6. **Balance revenue and profitability**  
   Business decisions should consider both sales revenue and profit rather than focusing on sales alone.
